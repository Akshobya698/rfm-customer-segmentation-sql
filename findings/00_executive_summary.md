# Executive summary — RFM Customer Segmentation Analysis

Marketing asked who our best customers are: how recently they buy, how
often, and how much. From 10,000 registered customers and 40,000 orders
(16 Mar–14 Jun 2026), 8,438 paid customers place 37,822 paid orders worth
Rs 28.3 crore. Detail lives in [01_customer_overview](01_customer_overview.md),
[02_rfm_distributions](02_rfm_distributions.md) and
[03_segment_definitions](03_segment_definitions.md).

- **A small core funds the business.** 966 Champions (11% of customers)
  drive 51.7% of revenue, averaging 19 orders and Rs 1,51,328 each.
  Together with 643 Loyal Heavy Repeaters, under 20% of customers produce
  almost 65% of revenue.
- **Most customers buy once and stall.** 4,679 customers (55%) ordered
  exactly once, contributing only 12.4% of revenue. Of those, 2,135 bought
  within the last six weeks and can still convert; 2,544 went quiet 44+
  days ago and rarely return.
- **Averages lie here.** Median spend is Rs 12,091 against a mean of Rs
  33,522, with the top 1% above Rs 3.15 lakh. Any readout built on the
  "average customer" will mislead; segments must separate whales from the
  base before averaging anything.

Recommendation: protect Champions with insider treatment, push Growing
Repeaters (25% of customers, 23% of revenue) toward a second category,
run a time-boxed second-purchase trigger on Recent One-Timers, and cap
At-Risk One-Timer win-back at one cheap test. Details and campaign angles
per segment are in [03_segment_definitions](03_segment_definitions.md).

# Customer segments — who to campaign to, and how (Milestone 4)

We scored all 8,438 paid customers on Recency, Frequency and Monetary, then
collapsed the 125 possible score combos into five segments you can actually
run campaigns against. One line up front: 11% of customers drive 52% of
revenue, and 55% of customers have ordered exactly once. Everything below
follows from those two facts.

## 1. Champions — 966 customers (11%), 51.7% of revenue

Bought in the last ~26 days, 7+ orders each (average 19), average spend Rs
1,51,328. These are your regulars and your whales in one group. Treat them
like insiders, not targets. Campaign angle: early access to new
collections, private previews, and a thank-you tier that costs little but
signals status. Goal is retention and advocacy, not conversion — losing one
Champion costs as much as losing twenty one-timers.

## 2. Loyal Heavy Repeaters — 643 customers (8%), 12.8% of revenue

Also 7+ orders (average 8.7) at Rs 56,248 average spend, but missing the
Champion bar on recency or spend size. They buy often and steadily rather
than big. Campaign angle: subscription-style nudges, bundle offers and
replenishment reminders that protect their cadence. They are the most
likely Champions-in-waiting, so track which ones respond and graduate them.

## 3. Growing Repeaters — 2,150 customers (25%), 23.2% of revenue

Two to six orders each (average 4.1), average spend Rs 30,471, middling
recency. This is the promotion audience: they have proven they will come
back, but the habit is not locked in. Campaign angle: cross-sell and
category bundles that widen their basket, plus a second-category incentive
before their gap since last order stretches. Moving even a tenth of this
group up the frequency ladder is worth more than any one-timer programme.

## 4. Recent One-Timers — 2,135 customers (25%), 5.7% of revenue

Exactly one order, placed within the last ~6 weeks, average spend Rs 7,578.
The window to act is open but closing. Campaign angle: a single,
time-boxed second-purchase trigger — onboarding sequence, quick review
request with a return incentive, or a companion-product offer tied to what
they bought. Measure this group on second-order rate within 30 days, not
on immediate revenue.

## 5. At-Risk One-Timers — 2,544 customers (30%), 6.7% of revenue

Exactly one order, 44+ days ago, average spend Rs 7,397. The largest group
and the smallest per-head value. Campaign angle: a cheap, strictly capped
win-back test (one reminder, one modest incentive), then stop. Do not chase
this group with Champion-level budgets — most of it will not return, and
that is fine. Suppress non-responders from paid remarketing quickly.

## How to use this next

Fund the top first: Champions get protection budget, Growing Repeaters get
growth budget, Recent One-Timers get a conversion experiment with a hard
readout date. At-Risk One-Timers get one test and a clear stop rule.
Scoring detail for the record: R and M are quintiles (analysis date 15 Jun
2026, paid orders only); F uses a custom 1–5 map because 55% of customers
sit at exactly one order and true quintiles collapse.
